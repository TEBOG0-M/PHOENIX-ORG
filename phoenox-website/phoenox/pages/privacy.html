Privacy notice

How PHOENOX handles your personal information. Last updated 16 November 2026.

This website is a student demonstration project. No real payments are processed and no real person’s data should be entered.

What we collect

  • Account details: name, email address, phone number, address and (for volunteers) skills and availability you choose to provide.
  • Donations: amount, currency, frequency, the mission supported and your chosen contact details. If you give anonymously we do not ask for your name, email or phone number, and anonymous gifts cannot be set up as monthly debit orders.
  • Volunteer applications: the missions and dates you apply for, and the manager’s decision.
  • Messages you send through our contact form.

Why we collect it

To run your account, process donations and applications, send you updates about missions you follow, issue tax receipts, and keep volunteers and the people we serve safe.

Where it is stored

Accounts and data are stored with Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore). Access is restricted by security rules: you can see your own records; managers can see what they need to run missions.

Your rights

Under South Africa’s Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) you may ask to access, correct or delete your personal information. You can edit most details yourself on your profile page, or contact us at hello@phoenox.org.

Safeguarding

The people we serve are often survivors of exploitation. We never publish their identities, photographs or locations without explicit consent.
