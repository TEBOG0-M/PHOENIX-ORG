How volunteering works

Four steps from sign-up to the field

Create your profile

Register as a Field Volunteer and list your skills — first aid, counselling, translation, trades, cooking and more. You can edit them any time.

Pick missions & days

Browse open missions in your dashboard and apply for a day that suits you. You can join several missions, on different days.

Await approval

Our managers review each application. You’ll see Pending, Accepted or Declined in your dashboard and get notified by email.

Serve, then look back

When a mission’s dates pass, it closes and moves to your history — a lasting record of the lives you’ve touched.

One mission per day. To protect you from burnout and keep our teams reliable, you can’t be booked on more than one mission on the same calendar day. The portal checks this for you automatically.
